Field Plot · a redesign proposal for every screen of the app

The app becomes a plotting bench for broken GPS tracks.

Same skeleton, same honest data language, new skin. The whole interface now sits on faint graph paper — the native medium of plotted tracks. Structure is drawn with equipment-grade ink borders instead of shadows, buttons behave like keycaps, and the numbers that matter are set as timing-board numerals. The signal orange stays reserved for exactly one job: everything the app reconstructs. Structure, copy, testids and flows are untouched — this is a visual layer swap.

What changes, what stays

Six deliberate moves. Everything else — layout regions, copy, data semantics — is preserved exactly as shipped today.

Changes
Graph-paper field under every screen — the app reads as a surveyor's sheet.
Equipment borders: 1.5 px ink outlines on primary cards and the map plate; hairlines only inside.
Keycap buttons — orange with black text and a hard bottom edge that depresses on click.
Big Shoulders condensed numerals for hero stats and headings; Archivo carries the UI.
Active toggle segments turn signal orange (today they turn black).
Provenance chips lead with a color square — the Mixed chip gets a half-ink / half-signal split.
Stays
Layout skeleton: header, three-tab landing, map-dominant workspace with a sticky tools rail, details section below.
Every label, hint, empty state and footnote — the product copy is identical.
Map semantics: ink route, severity-shaded gap dashes, orange reconstruction, selection glow.
The five pinned anchors only — #FC4C02, #000000, #FFFFFF, #5A5A5A, #222222 and their alphas.
Local-first privacy posture, provenance everywhere, destructive = black.
The share card itself — already the palette's native expression.

Palette

Five anchors, each with one job. Every tint in the mockups is an anchor at reduced alpha — no foreign hue anywhere, including the map.

Signal #FC4C02 What the app reconstructs — repairs, estimates, active intent.
Ink #222222 Recorded truth — body text, structure, the route line.
Ink+ #000000 Hard surfaces — destructive actions, severe severity, the share card.
Paper #FFFFFF The field — cards, controls, breathing room.
Shade #5A5A5A Secondary — hints, labels, suspect gap dashes.
