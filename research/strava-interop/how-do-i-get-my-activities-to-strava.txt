Written by Marie Updated over 3 weeks ago Table of contents How to Get Your Activities to Strava Record With the Strava App Sync From a GPS Watch or Wearable Import From Another Fitness App or Platform Upload a File Manually Add an Activity Without a Device FAQs How to Get Your Activities to Strava Strava works with most GPS watches, fitness apps, and cycling computers on the market. There are four main ways to get an activity onto Strava: recording it directly in the app, syncing from a connected device, importing from another fitness platform, or uploading a file by hand. Pick the method that matches your setup below. Record With the Strava App The simplest way to get an activity onto Strava is to record it directly in the iOS or Android app. Start the app before you head out, and it tracks your route and stats in real time. When you finish, your activity uploads automatically. Learn more about recording an activity , or check our syncing troubleshooting guide if an upload gets stuck. Sync From a GPS Watch or Wearable If you record with a watch, bike computer, or other wearable, most devices sync to Strava automatically once they're connected. Some of the most common devices include: Garmin Apple Watch COROS Polar Suunto Wahoo Don't see your device? See the full list of compatible devices and apps , or search for it in our device integration directory . Import From Another Fitness App or Platform If you already track workouts somewhere else, you can usually connect that platform to Strava instead of starting over. Common platforms include: MapMyRun MapMyRide Nike Samsung Health TrainerRoad Zwift Peloton For anything not listed here, check the full compatibility list or see if a direct integration exists . Upload a File Manually If you have a workout file saved to your computer — for example, an export from Garmin Connect or an older device that no longer syncs — you can upload it directly. Strava accepts GPX, TCX, and FIT files up to 25MB, as long as the file contains actual workout data. Upload files at strava.com/upload/select . Add an Activity Without a Device If you did a workout without recording it, you can still log it by hand. Learn how in our guide to uploading manual activities . FAQs What if my device isn't listed here? Check the full list of compatible devices and apps . If your device can export GPX, TCX, or FIT files, you can always upload those manually. Why isn't my activity showing up after I synced? This is usually a connection or permissions issue. See our syncing troubleshooting guide for steps specific to your device. Can I upload old activities from my computer? Yes. Any GPX, TCX, or FIT file with workout data can be uploaded through the file uploader , even if it's from an activity you did months ago. Is there a size limit on file uploads? Yes, files must be 25MB or smaller. Related Articles Suunto App and Strava How Do I Merge or Combine Activities on Strava? How Do I Export My Strava Data?
