Learn how to merge or combine Strava activities using third-party tools like FitFileTools or GotoES, then delete the originals and upload the combined file. Written by Marie Updated over 2 weeks ago While there's no way to merge two or more activities together on Strava, there are third-party tools you can use to combine the activities. Follow these instructions from the Strava website to export the activity files in their original format. If the files are in .FIT format , use this site to combine the files. If the files are in .TCX, .FIT, or .GPX format , use gotoes to combine the files. Can also combine overlapping activity files. You'll need to delete the existing activities on Strava before uploading the merged file. Unfortunately, you will lose all the kudos and comments on those activities.
